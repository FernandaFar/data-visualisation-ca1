# Data Visualisation and Communication – CA1

## About the Project

This project is part of my Data Visualisation and Communication assignment. I worked with employment data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) to explore occupations related to AI, Data Science and Software Development in the United States.

The main dataset covers May 2023. I used Python and Jupyter Notebook to explore the data, prepare it and create different visualisations to understand employment levels and wages.

## What I Explored

I created five different types of visualisations:

- **Bar Chart:** Comparing employment levels.
- **Pie Chart:** Exploring the employment distribution.
- **Scatter Plot:** Comparing employment and hourly wages.
- **Box Plot:** Looking at the distribution of hourly wages.
- **Histogram:** Exploring how frequently different wage values occur.

## Employment Trends (2021–2023)

For the challenge, I used additional BLS datasets from 2021 and 2022, together with the 2023 dataset, to compare employment estimates over time.

I focused on three occupations:

- Software Developers
- Data Scientists
- Computer and Information Research Scientists

The results showed an increase in employment estimates across all three occupations. Data Scientists had the highest percentage increase, while Software Developers had the largest increase in the estimated number of employees.
